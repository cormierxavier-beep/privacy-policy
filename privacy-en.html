Data we process

The data below is stored in your account (Firebase database) so that the Application can work:

  • Account: email address, password (stored in hashed form by Firebase Authentication) and account identifier.
  • Portfolios: names, types, broker links, cash, invested capital, transactions (asset, ISIN, quantity, price, date, buy or sell), contributions and recurring contributions.
  • Tracking: watchlist, notes, price targets, dividends, net-worth entries.
  • Price alerts: target price, direction and history of triggered alerts.
  • History: daily value of each portfolio, used for charts and summaries.
  • Preferences: currency, chosen AI assistant, daily and weekly summaries. Language and theme are stored on your device only.
  • Notifications: your device's notification token, used to send alerts and summaries.
  • Technical: Application version, onboarding status, compatibility markers.
  • Subscription: subscription tier, Pro trial dates, end-of-trial deadlines and reminders, grace period. A copy of this information is kept server-side, together with dates used to limit abuse (rate limiting).

Biometrics (Face ID, fingerprint) are handled by your device: Track'R receives no biometric data.

Track'R contains no advertising, no advertising tracking and no usage analytics tool, and does not sell your data. The third-party services listed below may nevertheless process some technical information (for example your IP address) under their own practices.

Use of Your Personal Data

  • To provide and maintain the Service and manage Your Account.
  • To perform the contract related to Your subscription and manage Your access rights.
  • To send You notifications (price alerts, summaries, reminders about Your subscription or account) if You allowed them on Your device. Firebase may also send You a password-reset email at Your request.
  • Security and abuse prevention.
  • To manage Your requests to Us.

Retention of Your Personal Data

The Company will retain Your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy.

  • Account Information: retained for the duration of your relationship with the Service. Upon subscription cancellation, a 30-day grace period applies during which your data is retained in read-only mode before permanent deletion.
  • End of the free Pro trial: if, when your free Pro trial ends, you do not have a subscription and your account exceeds the limits of the free plan (more than 2 portfolios or more than 8 distinct assets), you have 30 days from the start of that period to subscribe or to bring your account back within those limits. You are informed by an in-app banner and, if you have allowed notifications, by notifications (when the period starts, then 7 days and 1 day before it ends). Before any deletion, we check again the state of your subscription and the content of your account: if you have subscribed or are back within the limits, the period is cancelled and nothing is deleted. Otherwise, your account and the associated data stored on our servers are automatically and permanently deleted within a few days after the stated deadline. A minimal deletion record (technical account identifier, plan, number of portfolios and assets, date and reason) is kept for 12 months for security and audit purposes and then deleted, with no other personal data.
  • Technical logs (Google Cloud): server functions produce technical logs (account identifier, dates). They are kept for 30 days, then deleted.
  • Crash reports (Sentry): kept for 30 days (data hosted in the European Union), then deleted.
  • Database backups: daily backups are made and kept for 30 days, then deleted. After an account is deleted, Your data therefore disappears from the backups at the latest 30 days later.

Deleting Your account and data

You can delete Your account at any time in the Application (Profile, “Delete my account”), after entering Your password. This deletes Your data stored in the database (portfolios, transactions, alerts, preferences, history), the associated server-side copy and Your authentication account. It cannot be undone.

What deletion does not do:

  • It does not cancel Your subscription with Google Play or the App Store: to stop payments, cancel it in Your store account.
  • It does not delete Your purchase profile at RevenueCat, which keeps purchase information linked to Your account identifier under its own policy.
  • A minimal record of the deletion (technical account identifier, plan, number of portfolios and assets, date and reason) is kept for 12 months for security and evidence purposes, then deleted.
  • Database backups (daily, kept for 30 days) still contain Your data until they expire: it disappears from them at the latest 30 days after the account is deleted.
  • Data already received by third-party services follows their own retention periods: technical logs and crash reports, 30 days; RevenueCat, under its own policy.

Subscriptions and Payments

Track'R offers optional Premium and Pro subscriptions.

  • Payments are processed exclusively through Google Play (Android) and the App Store (iOS). We do not collect or store any banking or payment information.
  • Subscriptions are monthly or annual and renew automatically, unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your Google Play or App Store account, or within the app via the “Manage Subscription” button.
  • A 7-day free Pro trial is available for new users, once per account.
  • Upon cancellation, a 30-day grace period applies before the account is permanently deleted; your data remains accessible in read-only mode during this period.
  • No refunds are granted for the current period, in accordance with Google Play's and Apple's refund policies.
  • Subscription management is handled by RevenueCat.

Third-Party Services

Track'R uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase / Google Cloud (authentication, database, server functions): hosting of all the data above. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: subscription management. Receives Your account identifier, Your Apple/Google purchases and receipts and Your subscription status. This data is pseudonymized (linked to an identifier, without Your email address). RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: subscription payment.
  • Expo (push notifications): receives Your device's notification token and the content of notifications (title and text: asset, price, change), then relays them through Apple's or Google's notification services. Expo Privacy Policy
  • Yahoo Finance: stock prices. Requests from Your device send it, among other things, Your IP address and the requested symbols; requests from our servers contain no account identifier.
  • Finnhub: search of financial instruments and news. Your device sends it, among other things, Your IP address and the text or symbol searched.
  • Sentry (hosted in the European Union): crash reports. A report contains the device model, the system and Application versions and the error trace. It contains neither Your email address nor Your account identifier; Track'R endeavors to filter out, before sending, email addresses, tokens and amounts that might appear in an error message. Retention: 30 days. Sentry Privacy Policy

AI Assistant Feature

Track'R includes an optional AI Assistant feature (Pro subscription). When You choose “Analyze with AI”, the Application prepares a text containing the asset's name and ticker, its price, its day change and Your average cost. You can edit it before sending. It is sent only after Your action, by opening in Your browser the AI service You chose (Perplexity, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or an https:// address of Your choice): the text is then carried in the web address that is opened, and may remain in Your browser history and in the service's logs.

Track'R does not contact any AI service itself and receives no answer. Track'R does not send Your email address, Your account identifier, the quantities You hold or the value of Your portfolios. How the AI service processes the text is governed by its own terms and privacy policy. AI answers can be inaccurate and do not constitute investment advice.
